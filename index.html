Problem

Engineers needed a phone app that talks to a vehicle over BMW's proprietary protocol: authenticated pairing, live telemetry, sending commands, and a full record of every frame in both directions. None of this exists as a Flutter package.

What I built

  • A native iOS CoreBluetooth stack that encodes and decodes LIN frames carried over GATT, with multi-key authenticated pairing.
  • A bridge from that stack to Flutter over platform channels, streaming real-time telemetry and commands, with TX/RX logging on every frame.
  • A BLE peripheral simulator that sends malformed frames and failure paths, so the app can be tested without the physical hardware.

Field note · performance

A list of images that crashed the app on scroll

Every pagination load made the app stutter, and then it crashed. Flutter Inspector showed it decoding 3048×3048 images to fill 120-pixel tiles, at full resolution, in memory.

The fix was to decode at display size. Memory dropped and the crash went away. I wrote it up on LinkedIn, where it got more than 23,000 impressions.

// decode at the size it's shown, not the size it's stored
final px = (120 * MediaQuery.of(context)
    .devicePixelRatio).round();

CachedNetworkImage(
  imageUrl: url,
  memCacheWidth: px,
  memCacheHeight: px,
);
